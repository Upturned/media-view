## Image viewer

Shows one image as large as possible, with its details alongside.

- **Fit to screen** by default: large images are scaled down, small ones shown at natural size. Browsers can't show TIFF: the viewer shows a copy, made in the background when the image is found (the top bar shows *TIFF 3 / 12* meanwhile) or right away if you open it first.
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel (0.1× to 10×) and **drag** to pan. Click the zoom label to go back to *Fit*.
- **Previous / next** follow the page you came from — its order and filters. The film strip at the bottom shows the neighbors; click one to jump to it. If the image on screen leaves that list (you tag it while browsing *Untagged*, unstar it in *Favorites*…), it keeps its place in the strip, greyed out, and previous / next carry on from there.
- **Slideshow** steps through the images on its own. Next to it: the interval (2–30 s, the same steps as in Settings), **⟳ loop** (start over at the end, or stop) and **⤨ shuffle** (random order, each image once per round). **Settings → Slideshow** sets them too.
- **Random** — in the bar at the top, in the controls, or **R** — picks another image from the same list. **Full** shows the image alone, full screen.
- **Right-click** the image for the grid's image menu: Open with…, Tags…, Add to collection…, Rename…, Move…, Copy…, Star, Cover of… and Recycle.
- **Info** shows the file name, its folder (click any part to go there), size, dimensions and dates, and lets you **star** it.
- **Open with…** opens the image in another program; **Rename**, **Move** (you stay on the list you were browsing, on the next image; the message offers **Go to** the album and **Undo**), **Cover of ▸** (the album or a folder above it) and **Recycle** act on it. Below them, add a short **description**.
- **Tags** panel: type a tag and press Enter to add it — `type:name` picks the type, and a new name offers to create the tag. The **×** on a tag removes it from the image (tags added by an implication have a lock instead). Press **T** from anywhere in the viewer to jump to it, and **Esc** to go back to the images.
- **Collections** panel: the lists the image is on, with its place in each — click one to open it, **✕** takes the image off that list (never deletes it). **+ Add to collection** puts it on another. Opened from a collection, the viewer steps through it, shows *3 / 12 in collection*, and **Cover of ▸** offers the collection too.
- **Keys:** ← → previous / next · + − zoom · 0 fit · F full · R random · S slideshow · T tags · Esc one step back (exit full screen → stop the slideshow → back to the page you came from) · Backspace back to the page you came from. **Tab** into the film strip: ← → move along it without changing the image, Enter shows that one, Esc goes back. The *Shortcuts* tab lists them all.
