## Collection

One collection's images, in the list's own order. Each frame shows its number on the list.

- **Drag** a frame to reorder it; drag one of a selection to move them all together. The bar in front of a frame shows where they'll land, and **→ to the end** puts them last. The message after it offers **Undo**.
- **Sort** by *Collection order* (the default), *Name* or *Size*. Reordering only works in collection order with no filter on (**☆ Favorites**, **name contains…** or the index) — the bar says so, with a button to go back.
- The numbers always show the list's own order, even when sorted by name or size.
- **Click** an image to open it: the viewer steps through the page's order. **⚄ Random** and **▶ Slideshow** (in list order) are in the header.
- With images selected, **− Remove from collection** takes them off the list — the files stay in their albums, and **Undo** puts them back in place. The rest of the yellow bar works as on any grid.
- **Right-click** an image for more, including **Cover of** this collection.
- **Edit** changes the name, the description (up to 500 characters) and the cover: *First image* (whatever is 01), or one you **Choose…**. **Delete** removes the list only; the images stay where they are, and **Undo** is offered for a few seconds.
- The index beside the grid works as everywhere; its **Collections** section shows which other lists these images are on.
