## Tag

Every image with this tag, with the tag's type, aliases and implications at the top.

- **Edit tag** changes its name or type, its aliases (and which one is the main name), what it implies, merges it into another tag, or deletes it.
- **Random** opens a random image with this tag.
- **Wiki page →** opens the tag's wiki page: its description, related tags and info fields.
- The grid works like an album's: sort, filter, the tag index beside it, and the selection bar.
- **Keyboard:** the arrows move a white ring between frames (it shows after the first key and hides when you move the mouse); Enter opens, Space selects, Shift + arrows extends the selection, Backspace goes up a level.
