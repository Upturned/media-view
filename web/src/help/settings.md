## Settings

- **Library** — see which library is open, **Open library…** to switch to another folder, or **New library…** to start one. Recent libraries are listed for quick switching; libraries on a disconnected drive are marked *Not found*.
- **Profiles** — every profile of this library. Yours can be renamed, get a picture or a password; the others can be switched to or deleted (their stars and tag links go; images, tags and collections stay). **New profile…** makes another. See the profile picker's help for passwords.
- **Grids** — what album, tag and search grids open with: columns, sort and its direction, grouping (none, by collection or by format), and whether the index is shown. Changing them on a grid changes them here too.
- **Slideshow** — how long each image stays, what happens at the end (*Start over* or *Stop*), and the order (*The list's order* or *Shuffle*). The viewer's slideshow controls change them too.
- Grid and slideshow settings, and the theme, belong to your profile: each person sharing the library has their own.
- **Theme** — this version comes with one style, **Darkroom**. **Check styles** shows previews of the other styles being considered.
