## Library Health

Compares your library folder with what the app remembers and lists what needs attention, by colour — click a summary card at the top, or a group's name, to review it:

- **Red — missing:** folders or images the app can't find. *Recreate* a folder (with its marker, and the folders inside it) or *Locate…* an image — the file you pick must be the very same image. *Forget* deletes what the app remembers about it, so it asks twice.
- **Amber — needs a decision:** changes made in Explorer (*Keep* or *Undo*), unclear moves (the image found and each image it could be, side by side: pick the one it really is), loose images and folders inside albums, guessed folder kinds (*Confirm*; a folder made empty in Explorer is guessed again once something is in it), image formats the app can't show yet (*Ignore* or *Record*) and other stray files (*Recycle* or *Ignore*). A folder directly in Images that holds only images *looks like an album*: it’s listed with the unmarked folders — *Move as album…* puts it inside a category.
- **Blue — for your information:** untagged images (*Review ▸* opens their own page), duplicates (keep one copy, merge them into one — the kept copy gets every tag and everyone's stars — or keep all copies as separate images; a copy starred by anyone can't be recycled, and with several profiles the page says who starred it), unused thumbnails (and unused TIFF viewer copies). Not counted on the badge — only a blue dot when they're all that's left.
- **White — notices:** videos, audio and texts the scan moved to their own folders (*OK* dismisses them), and the files you chose to ignore (*Not tracked*: *Track again* or *Record*).

Each group has a fix for the whole group (it asks first) and **Review ▸** for item by item; on the Review page, every group lists all its fixes for the whole group (duplicates: *Keep suggested*, *Merge all*, *Keep all copies*). **Apply to all** lists everything it can fix, by severity, with a checkbox each: red and amber ticked, blue for you to tick — untick a line, or a whole severity with its heading. It skips the items that need your choice. **Rescan** checks the library again; during a scan the page is dimmed and fixes wait.

At the bottom: how many files of each unsupported format were ignored or recorded so far, and the logs (**Open logs folder**, **Clear logs**).
