## Library Health

Compares your library folder with what the app remembers and lists what needs attention, by colour:

- **Red — missing:** folders or images the app can't find. *Recreate* a folder (with its marker, and the folders inside it) or *Locate…* an image — the file you pick must be the very same image. *Forget* deletes what the app remembers about it, so it asks twice.
- **Amber — needs a decision:** changes made in Explorer (*Keep* or *Undo*), unclear moves (pick which image it really is), loose images and folders inside albums, guessed folder kinds (*Confirm*), image formats the app can't show yet (*Ignore* or *Record*) and other stray files (*Recycle* or *Ignore*).
- **Blue — for your information:** untagged images, duplicates (keep one copy, or merge them into one), unused thumbnails. Not counted on the badge — only a blue dot when they're all that's left.
- **White — notices:** videos, audio and texts the scan moved to their own folders (*OK* dismisses them), and the files you chose to ignore (*Not tracked*: *Track again* or *Record*).

Each group has a fix for the whole group (it asks first) and **Review ▸** for item by item. **Apply to all** fixes every red and amber item that has a default and skips the ones that need your choice. **Rescan** checks the library again; during a scan the page is dimmed and fixes wait.

At the bottom: how many files of each unsupported format were ignored or recorded so far, and the logs (**Open logs folder**, **Clear logs**).
