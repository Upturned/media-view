## Hub

The starting page. It shows one card per module. For now only **Images** is active; Videos, Audio and Text-Writer are coming later.

- The name and location of the open library are shown at the top.
- Click **Images** to open the Images module.
- **Right-click** a card for **Open**, or anywhere else on the page for the list of modules.
- To switch to another library, go to **Settings**.
