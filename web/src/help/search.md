## Search

Searches the whole library. The **Search** tab of this window explains the syntax.

- **All** shows the first few results of each kind — images, albums, categories & sub-categories, tags — each with **See all**.
- **Images** shows every matching image, with sorting, the tag index and the usual selection actions.
- **Albums**, **Categories & sub-categories**, **Tags** and **Collections** are found by the words of your search. Tags you name (`#elf`) and collections (`@refs`) come first on their tabs, marked *in your search*.
- **Keyboard:** the arrows move a white ring between frames (it shows after the first key and hides when you move the mouse); Enter opens, Space selects, Shift + arrows extends the selection, Backspace goes up a level.
