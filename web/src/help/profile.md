## Profile

A profile's page. Yours shows your favorites too; other profiles' pages show their linked tags and sections, but not their favorites.

- **Images by me** — the images tagged with any tag linked to you as **creator**: your artist name, your studio, your pen name.
- **Featuring you** — the images tagged with any tag linked to you as **subject**: you as a model, your self-portrait tag, a character you play.
- **Link a tag…** adds a tag to a section. You can also link a tag from its wiki page (**This is me ▸**) or from any tag's right-click menu (**This is me · …**). A tag can be linked to one profile per role. **×** unlinks it.
- Tags implied by others count too: an image tagged with something that implies a linked tag shows up.
- **See all** opens the section as a search (each tag written with its type, like `~#artist:felipe_art`).
- **Rename…**, **Picture…** (any image in the library, shown as a square; or your initials on a color you pick) and **Password…** change your profile. To change another profile, switch to it.
