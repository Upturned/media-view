## Library

The front page of the Images module.

- **Inbox** comes first: where new images land before being filed into albums. It shows how many are waiting, how many arrived this week and how old the oldest one is. Click it to open the Inbox.
- **Categories** follow, a→z, each with its cover and image count. In the Darkroom style a category is a *rack*. Click one to open it; right-click for more.
- **+ New category** creates a new top-level folder.
- **+ Add images** sends images to the Inbox; you can also drag files from Explorer onto the page.
- Changes you make in Explorer show up on their own after a moment.
- **Random** in the top bar opens a random image — from the page you're on (an album, a tag, a collection, a search), or from the whole library.
- Formats: JPG (also .jpeg, .jfif, .jpe), PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, SVG (also .svgz), BMP, ICO and TIFF.
- **Keyboard:** the arrows move a white ring between the cards (it shows after the first key and hides when you move the mouse); Enter opens, Backspace goes up a level.
