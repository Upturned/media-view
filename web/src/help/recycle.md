## Recycle Bin

Images and folders you recycle wait here — with their stars and descriptions — until you restore or delete them.

- **Restore** puts an item back where it came from. If that place no longer exists, use **Restore to…** and pick a new one.
- **Delete** removes an item from disk for good, after asking. **Empty the bin** deletes everything in it.
- Select several items (the boxes on the left) to restore or delete them together.
- Starred images are never recycled: unstar them first.
