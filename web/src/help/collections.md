## Collections

Every collection in your library. A collection is an ordered list of images from anywhere — a chapter's references, a comic page by page, a wallpaper shortlist. Nothing is moved or copied, and an image can be on as many lists as you like.

- Each card is a **contact strip**: the cover as frame 01, the next four frames, and how many more there are.
- **Sort** by name, number of images or recently changed; **↑ / ↓** flips the order. **name contains…** filters the cards.
- **+ New collection** creates an empty list. Names are unique — capitals, and spaces vs. `_`, don't count.
- To fill a list, select images in any grid and press **+ Collection** in the yellow bar, or right-click an image → **Add to collection…**.
- Click a card to open the collection.
