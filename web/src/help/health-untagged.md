## Untagged images

Images outside the Inbox that have no tags yet, album by album. Ones that arrived from outside the app (copied in with Explorer, a sync tool…) are marked **NEW**.

- Type a tag in a row's field and press Enter — `type:name` picks the type. The row leaves the list (the message offers **Undo**).
- **Mark as seen** clears the NEW mark; the image stays on the list.
- **Leave untagged** takes the image off the list, for pictures you never mean to tag. **Left untagged (n)** at the bottom lists them, with **Put back on the list**.
- Select rows (the circle, or **Select all**) to do it in bulk, or to open **Tags…** for all of them.
- **Open as grid ↗** shows the same images as a grid.
