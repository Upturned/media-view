## Welcome

No library is open yet. A **library** is a folder you choose anywhere on your computer; it holds your media and everything the app knows about it (in a hidden `.mediaview` folder).

- **Open library…** — pick a folder that is already a library. If it isn't one yet, you're offered to create one there.
- **New library…** — pick a folder (empty or not) and turn it into a library. Existing files are kept.
- **Recent libraries** — click one to open it again.
