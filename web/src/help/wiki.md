## Wiki page

Everything about one tag: its description, its images, how it relates to other tags, and its info fields.

- **Ctrl + click** any tag to get here (middle-click opens it in a new tab).
- Links to other tags are underlined in their type's color. A link **dashed in amber** points to a tag that doesn't exist yet — click it to create the tag.
- **Images** shows the newest few; **See all →** opens the full gallery.
- **Related tags** are the ones most often on the same images, with the share of this tag's images that also have them.
- **Info** shows the type's custom fields; empty ones are hidden.
- **Edit tag** changes the name, type, aliases and implications, merges or deletes the tag.

### Editing

**✎ Edit page** (or **E**) edits the description, the cover and the fields together. Nothing is saved until **Save page** (**Ctrl + S**); **Discard** (**Esc**) drops the changes.

- Markdown: `**bold**` · `*italic*` · `## heading` · `- list` · `> quote` · `[text](https://…)`.
- Tag links: `[[name]]`, `[[type:name]]`, `[[type:name|text to show]]`. Type `[[` for suggestions: ↑ ↓ to choose, **Enter** or **Tab** to insert, **Esc** to dismiss.
- **Edit / Split / Preview** shows the text, the preview, or both.
- **Change cover…** and **Choose image…** open the image picker: the tag's own images first; type to search the whole library. Double-click uses an image right away.
- Dates can be a year, a year and month, or a full date: `1954`, `1954-07`, `1954-07-29`.
- Fields are defined per tag type in **Tag types › Custom fields**.
